01 / Profile

Executive Summary

Summary

I’m an organisational development, strategy and wellbeing professional with a PhD in urban political sociology and a deep interest in human behaviour, social systems and how organisations evolve.

My career spans Commonwealth and State Government, university research, international programs and consulting. Across these environments, I’ve worked at the intersection of people, policy, strategy and organisational change—helping organisations navigate complexity, build stronger systems and create better ways of working.

Focus

I focus on the relationship between people, culture and organisational performance.

I believe organisations work best when people understand their roles, feel supported and have the structures, leadership and relationships they need to succeed.

My work brings together organisational design, workforce wellbeing, governance and practical change to create environments that are effective and supportive of people.

Capabilities

  • Strategy and transformation: Turning complex challenges into clear priorities, practical plans and achievable change.
  • Organisational development: Improving structures, governance, accountability and ways of working so that people and teams can perform effectively.
  • Engagement and collaboration: Bringing stakeholders together, navigating different perspectives and building shared understanding.
  • Research and evidence: Connecting insights from human behaviour, social science and data to inform better decisions.
  • Implementation and improvement: Translating ideas into workable frameworks, operating models and sustainable improvements.
  • Workplace culture and wellbeing: Supporting psychologically safe, inclusive and resilient organisations through thoughtful systems and leadership.

I bring an interdisciplinary perspective, combining analytical thinking with creativity, emotional intelligence and an understanding of how people respond to change.

02 / Purpose

Philosophy & motivation

Healthy organisations are designed, not simply hoped for.

Sustainable performance depends on more than policies, processes and performance measures. It requires clear structures, inclusive leadership, meaningful relationships and a workplace culture where people feel valued, supported and able to contribute.

I believe people should be able to succeed at work without hiding who they are or fearing mistakes. Trust, psychological safety and accountability shape how people participate, learn and work together.

My motivation is to understand why organisations get stuck, then design practical systems, structures and ways of working that enable lasting improvement. Wellbeing influences retention, innovation and organisational resilience, and belongs at the centre of organisational decisions.

For me, organisational wellbeing and organisational effectiveness are fundamentally connected.
